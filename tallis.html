01 — Overview

The Brief

What Tallis needed to solve, who it's for, and the Dyson lens it was designed through.

Project Brief

Design a desk-scale personal climate solution that explores localized thermal comfort beyond traditional room-based systems — improving individual comfort in shared spaces without disrupting anyone else in the room.

Brand — Dyson

Dyson is defined by engineering-led innovation: function-driven form, visible precision, and a belief that better engineering leads to better living. The concept treats the device not as a product extension, but as a new reference point for Dyson's future climate-focused products.

Who

Someone who values elevated comfort while working — drawn to solutions that feel premium and efficient, while staying mindful of sustainable, responsible energy use.

02 — Research & Process

Finding the Form

The questions that shaped the brief, and the sketches that carried Tallis to its final geometry.

How Might We
  • Localized comfort.Home HVAC struggles near windows and exterior walls — how might we deliver precise control that follows the user, not the room?
  • Faster response.Central systems react slowly to small shifts — how might comfort respond instantly instead of waiting on room-wide changes?
  • Quiet operation.Fans and heaters are loud during calls and deep work — how might heating and cooling stay acoustically unobtrusive?
  • Less clutter.Bulky devices disrupt small workspaces — how might a compact object integrate into modern home offices?
Ideation & Refinement

The right-triangular-pyramid direction combined hard angles with rounded edges in a way that stayed geometrically proportional when divided within itself — symmetry that reflects a curated environment. The rubber handle and analog buttons were later removed in favor of a seamless capacitive touch display, once it was clear the object would remain stationary.

04 — How It Works

Two Sides of the Same Face

The same ceramic front panel drives both warmth and cool, switching modes without moving parts.

Radiant Heat

A ceramic heating element built into the front face radiates warmth directly outward, heating the space in front of it up to 95°F without needing to heat the surrounding air first.

Laminar Cool

In cooling mode, the same face releases a smooth, laminar stream of air — cooling the space around you evenly, without the turbulence or noise of a traditional fan.

Summary

This project shifts temperature control from the room to the individual — radiant heating, quiet operation, and a screenless interface deliver precise comfort without disrupting shared spaces. The result is a premium personal climate device that functions as both a performance tool and a refined desk object.
