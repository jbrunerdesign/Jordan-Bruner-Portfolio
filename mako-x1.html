01 — Overview

The Brief

What MAKO X1 needed to prove, and the brand attitude behind it.

Project Brief

Redefine the electric superbike by rejecting the sterile “appliance” look common to modern EVs, in favor of the raw, muscular presence of a traditional mechanical powerhouse — using advanced surfacing and structural honesty to build a silhouette that feels as visceral and high-stakes as a combustion-engine icon.

Brand — MAKO

MAKO represents the new American standard in velocity, engineered to shatter existing technological benchmarks through raw, calculated force. Every machine is a delivery system for extreme speed and high-fidelity mechanical execution, using newly developed radial-axial motors and immersion cooling to push the limits of what is physically possible — a commitment to performance dominance where uncompromising power defines the entire identity.

02 — Research & Direction

Chasing the Jesko

The reference that set the tone, and the engineering logic that shaped every surface.

Goals

Distill the Jesko's sense of absolute dominance and precision into a two-wheeled format without resorting to a literal copy-paste of its body panels — capturing that same mechanical intimidation, where every surface looks calculated to handle extreme forces, so the bike reads as an inevitable evolution of high-speed engineering rather than a styling exercise.

Engineering Logic
  • Mass & weight distribution.The battery is the primary mass driver, so it becomes the frame's central stressed member — a 245 kg target at a near 50/50 front-rear split keeps it from compromising lean-angle agility.
  • Wheelbase.At 1,440 mm the frame sits inside the 1,400–1,480 mm superbike range — long enough to tame wheelie tendencies from high torque, short enough to stay flickable through corners.
  • Wheel & tire.17-inch wheels front and rear, with a 120/70 front and 200/55 rear, give the widest choice of racing compounds and the contact patch needed to put 200+ Nm to the pavement without spin.
  • Steering geometry.A 24° rake and 100 mm of trail balance quick turn-in against high-speed stability — steeper angles feel twitchy, slacker ones dull the response.
  • Battery.An 800V immersion-cooled pack paired with silicon-carbide control sustains 160kW continuously without thermal throttling — 99% efficient and small enough to tuck into the bike's neck.
  • Motor.A scaled Raxial-flux “Dark Matter” motor delivers 160kW peak in a 260×120 mm pancake form — thin enough to kill aero drag, its added surface area improving oil-to-water heat exchange.

Summary

MAKO X1 treats a motorcycle's silhouette as a direct read-out of its performance — a Raxial-flux motor, an 800V immersion-cooled pack, and a chassis tuned specifically for high-torque EV delivery, wrapped in surfacing that communicates its numbers before the throttle is ever touched.
