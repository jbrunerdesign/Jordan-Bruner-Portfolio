01 — Overview

The Concept

What the Celestiq Band is, and the Cadillac language it speaks.

Concept

The Celestiq Band is a biometric wrist wearable that serves as a personalized access key for the Cadillac Celestiq. Its geometric face displays essential vehicle status while keeping interaction minimal and intuitive — designed as both technology and fashion, merging luxury engineering with the presence of a refined accessory.

Brand — Cadillac

Cadillac is an American luxury brand defined by bold design, advanced technology, and a legacy of craftsmanship. Its modern identity blends heritage with a forward-looking vision, emphasizing innovation without losing its iconic sense of presence — comfort, quiet power, and a distinctly architectural design language. With the emergence of its ultra-luxury flagship models, the brand now places greater focus on hand-built artistry and bespoke experience: a new era of American luxury, precise, expressive, and unapologetically refined.

Who

The target user is a Cadillac Celestiq owner who values craftsmanship, advanced technology, and effortless luxury — expecting products that blend performance with quiet sophistication, never loud or overdesigned. This user appreciates seamless interaction: access that recognizes them, responds instantly, and requires no friction. Their lifestyle is defined by exclusivity and personalization, where objects function as extensions of identity rather than status displays.

Mission

To create a seamless, biometric access experience that elevates the relationship between driver and vehicle. The Celestiq Band turns identity into the key itself — delivering effortless entry, intuitive status feedback, and a level of personalization worthy of Cadillac's flagship. Designed as both technology and jewelry, it aligns luxury, fashion, and innovation into a single, understated form.

05 — How It Works

Four Points of Contact

Every function lives at the edge of the band, mapped to a single, deliberate touchpoint.

Fingerprint Scanner

Set into one side of the band, the fingerprint scanner authenticates the wearer before any other function activates — the same biometric signature that unlocks the phone becomes the key to the car.

Function Button

The opposite side houses a single multi-purpose button, cycling through lock, unlock, remote start, and other vehicle functions with short and long presses instead of a cluttered array of controls.

Ambient Crystal Face

The faceted crystal face glows in different colors and patterns to signal status at a glance — locked, charging, connected, or in need of attention — without a single pixel or screen.

Heart Rate Sensor

On the underside, a heart rate sensor continuously reads the wearer's vitals and relays them to the vehicle, letting the car adjust cabin temperature, seat massage, and ambiance for maximum comfort before the driver even sits down.

01 — Overview

The Ceremony

What the packaging needed to say, and who it was made for.

Concept

This package is not an unboxing, it is a ceremony. Every layer is designed to reveal itself slowly and intentionally, treating the act of opening as the first expression of ownership. The kit communicates a singular truth: that what lives inside was made for exactly one person.

Persona

The recipient is someone who has already decided that ordinary is not an option — they have spent months collaborating with Cadillac's design team to configure every material, finish, and detail of a vehicle built exclusively for them. This package arrives as the first physical proof of that decision, a tangible bridge between the moment of commission and the moment of delivery.
